KDAG is the Machine Learning society at IIT Kharagpur — we run projects, competitions, and workshops for anyone curious about data, from complete beginners to Kaggle regulars.

This bootcamp is our entry point. Over three sessions you'll write your first Python scripts, wrangle a real dataset with Pandas, and walk away with a chart and a conclusion you found yourself — no prerequisites beyond curiosity.

Who it's for
First and second-year undergrads, any department
Format
Live, in-person sessions with take-home exercises
What to bring
A laptop — we'll set up Python together on day one

What you'll learn

Each module builds on the last, so by the final session you're analysing data end to end.

01

Python Fundamentals

Variables, loops, functions, and enough syntax to stop fearing a blank script.

02

NumPy & Pandas

Loading, cleaning, and reshaping real tabular data without losing your mind.

03

Data Visualization

Turning a dataframe into a chart that actually answers a question.

04

Intro to ML

A first look at how a model learns, using a dataset you already understand.

Three evenings, start to finish

DAY 1

DAY 2

DAY 3
